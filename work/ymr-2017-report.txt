ABOUT YOUNG MINISTERS' RETREAT 2017 (YMR 2017)
YMR is a mountain for revival for young minsters passionate to see the kingdom of
God invade the world in all fields. It is a gathering of young, passionate men and
woman of God to pray and seek the face of God for fire needed to change the world's
system to God's system.
YMR 2017 is a 72 hour retreat of fasting, praying and seeking the face of God for the
fire needed for impact. YMR is a new generation Azuza revival.
It is organized by the Living Seed Church of the Redeemed Christian Church of God.
The parish was set up by the Pastors Seed Family in November, 2016.
Vision/
To bring young men and women together,
setting them on fire for impact in their generation.
